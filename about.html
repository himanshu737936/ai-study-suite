What it is

AI Study Suite PRO is a free study tool that turns the material you already have — an NCERT PDF, a standard book, your own notes, or a photo of a textbook page — into exam-ready practice: multiple-choice questions in the real exam pattern, spaced-repetition flashcards, a mind map, a one-page cheat sheet, plus a mains answer evaluator and teach-back mode.

It is built for aspirants of UPSC Civil Services, UPPCS and other State PSC exams, SSC, Railway, Banking and state police exams. Read the full walkthrough on the guide page.

How it is made

It is a single self-contained web page. There is no backend and no build step: everything runs in your browser, PDF parsing and OCR happen on your device, and you bring your own AI key. The whole thing is published openly on GitHub, so you can read the code, fork it or report a bug.

It leans on excellent open-source work: PDF.js for reading PDFs, Tesseract.js for on-device OCR, and jsPDF for exporting scorecards and notes. Thank you to those projects.

Accessibility statement

We want the app to be usable by as many people as possible. It includes an accessibility panel (the ♿ button in the header) with:

  • Text size — three steps, for easier reading.
  • Dyslexia-friendly font and a high-contrast mode.
  • Reduce motion — turns off animations, including the animated galaxy background.
  • An animated-background on/off switch, for comfort and battery.

Beyond the panel, the app supports keyboard navigation with visible focus outlines, screen-reader labels on icon buttons, a live region that announces status messages, and it respects your operating system's "reduce motion" setting. If you hit an accessibility barrier, please email us and we will try to fix it.
