Turn a PDF — or a photo of a book page — into UPSC / UPPCS / SSC practice

AI Study Suite PRO takes the study material you already have — an NCERT PDF, a standard book, your own notes, or even a photo of a textbook page — and turns it into exam-ready practice: multiple-choice questions in the real exam pattern, spaced-repetition flashcards, a mind map, and a one-page cheat sheet.

How to use it — step by step

  1. Get a free AI key. Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek or Mistral all work. Gemini and Groq have generous free tiers.
  2. Paste the key. The app detects the provider automatically, probes which models your key can use, and ranks them. You never pick a model manually.
  3. Upload your material. Drop in a PDF, or select photos of book pages — the app reads them with on-device OCR.
  4. Choose the exam and the mode. Pick UPSC / UPPCS / SSC, how many questions, the language (English, Hindi or Hinglish), and whether you want a quiz, flashcards, a mind map or a cheat sheet.
  5. Press Generate. Questions are written strictly from your PDF, spread across the pages you selected.
  6. Revise and track. Use the mistake notebook, adaptive practice on weak topics, the weakness heatmap and the daily revision plan.

Why the answers can be trusted

Most quiz generators let the AI answer from general knowledge, which is how you end up with questions that are not in your book at all. This app is built the other way around:

  • Grounded in your page. Each question must cite the page number and quote the sentence that proves the answer. The app checks that quote against the real page text and marks it verified, partial or unverified.
  • High-accuracy mode (on by default). After a quiz is generated, every question is re-checked blind — the marked answer is hidden — by two independent samples, ideally one from your main provider and one from a second provider. Where they disagree, the question is re-decided from the source text and corrected automatically.
  • Confidence badges. Every question shows 🎯 High confidence, 🟡 Medium, 🔧 Auto-corrected, or ⚠️ Low confidence.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Study Suite PRO free?

Yes. The app itself is free and open — there is no subscription and no server of ours. You only need a free API key from an AI provider (Gemini and Groq both have free tiers), and the app auto-selects the best model your key can use.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. There is an optional local account (a name and a password kept only on your own device) if you want one, but it is not required.

Does it work with a photo of a book page?

Yes. You can upload photos (JPG or PNG) of textbook pages; the app reads the text with on-device OCR and builds the questions from that text.

Is my PDF or my data uploaded anywhere?

Your file stays on your device — PDF parsing and OCR happen in the browser. Only the extracted text is sent to the AI provider you chose, and your API key goes straight to that provider. There is no server in the middle.

Which AI providers are supported?

Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek and Mistral. Paste any of their keys and the app detects the provider by itself. OpenAI blocks browser requests, so Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek or Mistral are the smoothest options.

Can it make questions in Hindi?

Yes — choose English, Pure Hindi (Devanagari) or Hinglish, and the questions, explanations and cheat sheets come out in that language.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded it is installable as an app and its shell works offline, but generating questions needs an internet connection because the AI runs in the cloud. The PDF parsing and OCR never need the network.
