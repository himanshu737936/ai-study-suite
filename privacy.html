AI Study Suite PROPrivacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

AI Study Suite PRO has no server of its own. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not create a profile of you. Your PDFs and photos are processed on your own device. Only the text extracted from your file is sent to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key. You can erase everything at any time from the app.

1. Who we are

AI Study Suite PRO ("the app", "we") is an independent, free study tool built by Himanshu. It is a single web page that runs entirely in your browser. Because there is no backend, there is no database of users and no account on our side.

2. Information we collect

We collect no personal information on any server. We have no servers that receive your data. There is no sign-up, no email collection, no user database, and no usage tracking.

3. What is stored on your own device

To remember your settings and progress, the app saves data in your browser's local storage and in your browser's IndexedDB database. This never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. It includes:

Stored on your deviceWhy
Your API key(s), if you tick "Remember key"So you do not have to paste it every time. If you also enable the vault, the key is encrypted with a passphrase you set.
Your optional local account (name and a salted password hash)To let you sign in on this device. The password is stored only as a PBKDF2 hash, never as plain text.
Study data — generated sets, flashcards and their review schedule, mistakes, doubts, progress, statistics, exam date, revision planSo your revision history and weak-topic analysis survive a reload.
PDF Library filesStored in your browser's IndexedDB so you do not have to re-upload them.
Preferences — theme, language, font size, accessibility options, galaxy on/offSo the app looks and behaves the way you set it.

If you do not tick "Remember key", your API key is kept only for the current browser tab and is discarded when you close it.

4. What leaves your device, and to whom

When you press Generate (or use the doubt, mains, teach-back or visual tools), the following happens:

  • The text extracted from the pages you selected is sent, over HTTPS, directly from your browser to the AI provider whose key you pasted — for example Google (Gemini), Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek or Mistral.
  • Your API key is sent to that same provider as an authorisation header. It is never sent to us.
  • If you use the optional Gemini text-to-speech feature, the text you ask it to read is sent to Google for that purpose.
  • The AI provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle that text. Please read it — we do not control it and cannot delete data held by them.

Your original PDF or photo is never uploaded anywhere. PDF parsing and photo OCR happen inside your browser.

5. Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookies and includes no analytics, no pixels, no advertising and no fingerprinting. There is nothing to consent to, and there is no cookie banner because there are no cookies.

6. Hosting and libraries

Like any website, the app is served by a host and loads a few open-source libraries from public content networks. These providers may see your IP address and basic request information in their normal server logs:

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages, and mirrors on Cloudflare Pages and Netlify.
  • Libraries: cdnjs (Cloudflare), jsDelivr and unpkg — used for PDF reading, OCR and PDF/Word export.
  • OCR language data: loaded from the Tesseract data host only when you use a photo.

These are ordinary web requests; we do not receive or store anything from them.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a general study tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

8. Your control over your data

  • Clear everything: use "Clear all saved data" in the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Sign out / delete your local account: the Account screen has a delete option that removes the account from the device.
  • Keys: turn off "Remember key", or use the key-lock button to wipe keys from memory.
  • Browser-level: clearing your browser's site data removes everything the app stored.
  • Data held by your AI provider: contact that provider directly; it is outside our control.

9. Security

The app is served over HTTPS. Saved keys can optionally be encrypted with AES-GCM under a passphrase you choose, and the local account password is stored only as a PBKDF2 hash. That said, anything stored in a browser can be read by someone who has access to that device, so please do not use the app on a shared computer with "Remember key" switched on.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will change, and the current version always lives at this URL.
